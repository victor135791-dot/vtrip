Private iCloud Backup & Restoring on a New Phone

vTrip stores an automatic encrypted backup snapshot in your personal Apple iCloud Private Database so you never lose your trips if you replace or lose your iPhone.

  • Enable or check status: open Settings → iCloud Private Backup to verify your last backup date, trigger Back up now, or turn automatic backups on or off.
  • Restore on a new phone: sign into the same Apple Account on your new iPhone, install vTrip, and open the app. vTrip automatically detects your private iCloud backup and offers a one-tap Restore from iCloud prompt. You can also restore anytime from Settings → iCloud Private Backup → Restore from iCloud (choosing Merge or Replace if you already created trips locally).
  • Gmail reconnection: for your security, Gmail OAuth tokens stay strictly on each device and are not uploaded to iCloud. After restoring your trips and saved confirmations on a new phone, simply reconnect Gmail in Booking inbox if you want to check for new confirmations.

Connecting Gmail & Importing Bookings

  • Open Reservations → Booking inbox and tap Add Gmail account.
  • You can connect multiple Gmail accounts and switch between them without signing out.
  • Choose an email lookback window (1 month up to 5 years), filter by country or booking type, review extracted dates and prices, and select only the bookings you want to add to a trip.
  • Prefer not to connect Gmail? Tap Paste confirmation to import bookings manually without connecting any email account.

Opening Original Emails in Apple Mail

Inside Booking inbox, tap View original email guide for step-by-step instructions. Adding your Gmail account to iOS Apple Mail (iPhone Settings → Apps → Mail → Mail Accounts → Add Account) lets vTrip open the exact original confirmation email with one tap from any saved booking.
